Open positions

Executed (simulated fill) bets awaiting settlement. Entry price and exposure are immutable records. Rows carrying a quarantine flag are shown with that flag: they are not counted in exposure or ranking P&L.

Mark to market

The spec requires a current price for every open position. It is only shown when one was actually observed — never estimated. 14 positions are open: 14 line-only.

ESPN's free feed publishes spreads and totals as lines without side prices (price_format='line', price=0.0), so for those bets the honest mark is the line, not a price: the line move column is real observed movement since the decision timestamp. Reading that 0.0 as a price would be fabrication, so it is excluded. Unrealized P&L is computed only where a real price exists; it is currently UNAVAILABLE for all 14 positions.

Flagged and quarantined bets (kept, published, never hidden)

A bet is quarantined while it carries a critical defect flag that is in force — a decision that read rolling state older than the freshness limit, or a claimed edge above 20% that indicates a broken input rather than an edge. Quarantined bets are never deleted; they are excluded from the competition's exposure, P&L and ranking, because a bet priced off a broken input is not a measurement of the strategy. Currently excluded: 10 of the 14 flagged bets — settled 0 bets / P&L +$0.00, open 10 ($262.58 stake).

A flag can also be retracted: when a check turns out to have described a defect the decision did not have, a second row (<flag>-retracted) is appended and the bet counts again. The original row is never edited or deleted — bet_flags is append-only at the database level, with triggers that abort UPDATE and DELETE — so both the mistake and its correction stay on this page. On 2026-09-22 four NBA-026 spread bets were released this way: the stale-state check had measured team_gamelogs age for a rule that reads only Elo and the observed spread.
