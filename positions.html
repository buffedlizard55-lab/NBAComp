Quarantined bets (kept, flagged, excluded from ranking)

A bet is quarantined when it was placed on a decision state that is provably invalid — team state older than the 14-day freshness limit, or a claimed edge above 20% that indicates a broken input rather than an edge. Quarantined bets are never deleted and their settlement is still recorded here. They are excluded from the competition's exposure, P&L and ranking, because a bet priced off a broken input is not a measurement of the strategy.
