Rest Edge Raven NBA-001 v1.0.0 Rest & Scheduling awaiting-opportunity

plays as @RestEdgeRaven

Thesis. Teams on the second night of a back-to-back underperform relative to market prices when facing a rested opponent; markets underreact to rest differentials.

Rules. Bets against a team playing its second game in two nights on the road against an opponent with 2+ rest days, using game-winner contracts when the model edge exceeds threshold.

  • home team rested >= 2 days
  • away team on 2nd night of B2B (rest_days==0)
  • model_prob - market_prob >= 0.04
